# 📸 Application Screenshots

## 🏠 Dashboard

![Dashboard](screenshots/dashboard.png)

The Dashboard provides users with a comprehensive overview of their broadband connection, including internet status, active package, package expiry countdown, monthly data usage, connected devices, outstanding bills, and quick actions such as Speed Test, Restart Router, and AI Assistant.

---

## 📦 Internet Packages

![Internet Packages](screenshots/packages.png)

The Internet Packages page allows customers to browse available broadband plans, compare speeds, pricing, and subscription details. Users can upgrade, renew, or compare packages through an intuitive interface.

---

## 📊 Usage Analytics

![Usage Analytics](screenshots/usage-analytics.png)

The Usage Analytics page presents interactive charts and visual reports showing daily, weekly, and monthly internet usage. It helps users monitor data consumption, identify peak usage hours, and understand their internet activity.

---

## 💳 Bills & Payments

![Bills & Payments](screenshots/billing.png)

The Billing & Payments section enables users to view invoices, payment history, outstanding balances, tax details, and manage Auto-Pay. Users can securely pay their broadband bills online.

---

## ⚡ Internet Speed Test

![Speed Test](screenshots/speed-test.png)

The Speed Test feature measures download speed, upload speed, ping, and overall network performance. It provides users with real-time internet quality insights to diagnose connectivity issues.

---

## 🤖 AI WiFi Assistant

![AI Assistant](screenshots/ai-assistant.png)

The AI WiFi Assistant offers intelligent customer support by answering broadband-related questions, providing troubleshooting guidance, recommending internet packages, and assisting users with billing, router management, and account-related tasks through a conversational interface.
